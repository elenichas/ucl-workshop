# PROGRESS.md · a template for your log

Copy this into your project folder as `PROGRESS.md`, or simply ask Claude at the start:

```text
Keep a PROGRESS.md in this folder. After each step, add an entry:
what I asked, what you changed (in plain words), what we decided,
and what's next. Read it at the start of every new chat.
```

Why bother? A new chat starts with an empty memory. This file is how you, and Claude, remember what happened and why.
